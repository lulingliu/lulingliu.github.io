Luling Liu is a PhD Candidate at the Graduate School of Environmental Studies, Nagoya University. Her research interests lie at the intersection of remote sensing, energy economics, and sustainable development. She specializes in utilizing multi-source earth observation data, particularly nighttime lights, to monitor spatiotemporal dynamics of energy poverty and socioeconomic inequality.
